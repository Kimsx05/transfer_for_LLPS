# Composition summaries

Center composition = each center spot's posterior-mean abundance divided by its included-cell-type total. Neighborhood composition = sum of abundances across distinct observed spots within the selected radius, divided by the neighborhood total.

Pooled composition = mean of these per-center profiles over spots assigned to each niche, not a total cell-count estimate. Section-balanced = first mean profile within section×niche, then equal-section mean. Patient-balanced = equal-section means within each verified patient×niche, then equal-patient mean. Unknown patient identities are not treated as singleton patients.

All 30 subtypes retain their original9-major mapping; Mural is separate from Fibroblast. Niche labels are shared across sections; numeric IDs of different models do not assert equivalent biology. Central profiles are saved separately from the neighborhood profiles used as clustering features.
