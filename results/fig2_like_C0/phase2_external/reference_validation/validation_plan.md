# Prespecified reference validation plan (seed 123)

Recorded before inspecting prediction performance. CIBERSORTx is not configured with an account/token in this environment. The alternative is MuSiC's official `music.iter` / `music.basic` weighted NNLS implementation, preserved unchanged with its GPL license. It models linear RNA mixtures using cell-type mean library size and cross-patient expression variance. It is not equivalent to CIBERSORTx S-mode and does not establish microarray batch correction.

Reference contains frozen C0–C6 and Epithelial, Endothelial, Myeloid, T_NK, B, Plasma, Mast. Previously excluded stromal cells and unresolved major Cycling remain excluded; these omissions limit transfer to real bulk. Raw counts are aggregated once to sample×type and then patient×type. Training profiles require >=20 cells per patient/type and at least 3 training patients per type; a missing type invalidates that fold, never drops it silently. The MuSiC sufficient statistics (mean relative expression, across-patient variance, mean UMI per cell) are computed from these aggregates, with equivalence checked against the upstream basis code on a deterministic small fixture. No phase 1 DEG gene list is used for training.

All five datasets are held out in turn. Every specimen/cell of held-out patients is absent from training. Main natural mixtures are observed summed raw counts of all retained reference types in one preselected specimen per held-out patient (largest total retained cell count, tie lexical sample ID). All candidate sample predictions remain available for audit. At least 50 retained stromal cells are required for the primary C0-fraction accuracy evaluation; smaller denominators are separately descriptive. No test labels guide gene selection or model parameters. Genes require training mean >=1 CPM in at least one type; variance is estimated with patient as replicate. MuSiC defaults: iter.max=1000, nu=0.0001, eps=0.01, no centering, no normalization.

The target is predicted C0 / sum(predicted C0..C6), with all coefficients from the same fit and positive predicted stromal denominator. Report C0/C5 fraction, Pearson/Spearman, MAE, RMSE, signed bias, per-dataset metrics, and held-out pure-type confusion. Pure-type controls are diagnostic, not realistic bulk validations. Full-rank/conditioning and convergence are recorded.

Operational gate for abundance-based clinical use: overall independent-patient Spearman >=0.70, MAE <=0.15, RMSE <=0.20, |bias| <=0.10; evaluable datasets (>=5 qualifying patients) should have MAE <=0.20 and no consistent reversed association. For held-out C0/C5 controls require median correct-type fraction >=0.70 within C0–C6 and median reciprocal misassignment <=0.20. These pragmatic thresholds are not published universal standards. Strong correlation alone cannot compensate for a wrong scale.

Platform sensitivity uses the measured gene coverage of GPL570/GPL6947/GPL6102, plus fixed log2 gene-efficiency perturbations (SD=0.5) generated independently of cell fractions, and optionally gene-length normalization for RNA-seq. Such perturbations stress the model but are not true matched bulk validation. Cross-platform clinical abundance claims require no material degradation; even passing this screen cannot prove transfer to actual microarrays. If identity discrimination or numerical calibration fails, stop abundance-based Fig2E/F, retain negative validation figures and tables, and do not search for significance with replacement methods/cutoffs.
