# Frozen exploratory design
Seed 123; serial BLAS/R, maximum four CPU affinity. Original scores read-only. Primary Five UCell vs LLPS77 UCell; AUCell and A26/core sensitivity. Present features only; maxRank1500; AUCell ceil(5% full section feature universe). Common LLPS set is coverage sensitivity, not calibrated LLPS activity. High coverage >=90%. High masks strictly above 95/90/80th percentile (ties reduce selected fraction).
Rank-residual adjustment: Fibroblast, UMI, nFeature, Epithelial. Matching without replacement with per-covariate percentile caliper0.1, before/after pooled-SD SMD; balance requires >=50% high matched, >=10 pairs, all absolute SMD<=0.1. Two- and three-covariate matching reported. Rings0–3 graph steps; epithelial fraction threshold0.3 (0.2/0.4 sensitivity). HH controls Five-high/LLPS-not-high, matching composition.
Spatial-null sensitivity: random hex-lattice translations of LLPS map, compare only observed overlap >=50% original spots, exclude zero shift; 199 shifts. Preserves translated local values/geometry on overlap, not full irregular tissue boundary; report overlap and use as exploratory sensitivity, not definitive spatial P. No iid spot-based inference. Patient aggregation within dataset/patient, median and1000 patient bootstrap; unknown patients excluded, section descriptions retained.
Sparse-hotspot diagnosis: positive-score-only correlations and top thresholds, exact Five leave-one-gene-out UCell contributions; do not interpret higher Moran as specificity. Formal C5 NO-GO preserved.

Before full-result inspection: supplementary patient-level two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on aggregated section effects, only >=5 known patients and nonzero effects. Assumes approximately symmetric effect distributions; exploratory, not proof of source generalization. BH families separately comprise all ALL_KNOWN contrasts within association, rings, and epithelial-distance tables, including sensitivity subsets. Dataset/LODO p-values are diagnostic only and not labeled FDR. Bootstrap CIs are pointwise, not simultaneous. HH-versus-control LLPS-score separation is selection-defined and must not be interpreted as a discovery.

Additional diagnostic sensitivities specified during technical pilot, before complete-result inspection: Fib-high stratum additionally adjusted for Fib/UMI/nFeature/Epi; all-nine-major abundance adjustment; Fib/Epi q05 adjustment; adjusting for Five and LLPS detected gene counts. These are overadjustment-sensitive diagnostics, not identification of cell-intrinsic biology. Report all and do not select whichever yields significance.
